One word, and nothing else: the single word Grandma would text about AI.

Grandma doesn't say "artificial intelligence". She doesn't say "robot" either —
she squints at the phone, taps the search bar she has used for twenty years,
and calls the whole thing by the only name she has ever given it: **Hogwash**.

Warm, a little baffled, and instantly hers — the word she reaches for when the
machine talks nonsense, the way she would type it to a grandchild.

## Entry

Running `main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the entry word to
`/output/entry.txt`:

```
Hogwash
```

One word, 7 characters, UTF-8, no trailing newline.
